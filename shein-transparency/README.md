# SHEIN DSA transparency reports

Browser-fetched source workbooks from SHEIN's [Digital Services Act hub](https://euqs.shein.com/digital-service-act-a-1994.html).

- `raw/shein-2025-h1.xlsx` covers 1 January through 30 June 2025.
- [`../shein.xlsx`](../shein.xlsx) covers 1 July through 31 December 2025 and is
  the publisher's corrected 30 July 2026 version used by the VLOP converter.

The corrected H2 workbook supersedes every earlier version. SHEIN reduced the
out-of-court dispute count from 50 to 45 after finding that five January and
February 2026 cases had been pulled into the July to December 2025 period. It
also corrected the share of outcomes implemented from 1% to 100%. Both figures
refer to the same single implemented outcome, so that underlying result did not
change.

SHEIN says it republished through the correction process in Article 1(2) and
Annex II, section 5 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2835. It
also says it strengthened its extraction process to prevent repeats. The
workbook discusses narrower EU27 filtering and closer use of the harmonised DSA
definitions, but says those methodology changes were already present in the
original report and later versions.
